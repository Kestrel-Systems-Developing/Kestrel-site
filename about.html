Who we are

Reliable, well-engineered systems.

Kestrel Systems designs and delivers dependable technology systems for businesses that value clarity, speed and precision.

We observe closely, plan carefully and execute without waste. We build products we can stand behind — a learning management system, a QR management system and a school management system — and we offer them in the way that suits you: installed on your own servers, or hosted by us.

Whichever you choose, you deal directly with the people who built the software, with clear scopes and no surprises.
