Questions

Frequently asked.

What does the six months of technical support cover?

Installation help, bug fixes, security patches and answers to deployment questions for six months after handover. New features are scoped separately unless you chose the customization plan.

Do dynamic codes still work if we self-host?

Yes. Redirects are served from your own domain and servers, so your codes do not depend on a third party.

What happens to printed codes if we change plans?

Codes keep working. Moving between hosting options is handled by redirecting your domain, so nothing needs reprinting.

How is customization scoped?

We run a short discovery workshop, write a specification with an agreed number of hours, then deliver in short iterations you can review.

What happens after the support period ends?

The software keeps running; your licence does not expire. You can renew a support plan for continued updates and help.
